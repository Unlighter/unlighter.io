■ The Kraken
🇺🇸
🇫🇷
Tuesday, September 8, 2026

« The world used to be a bigger place »
« The world is still the same. There's just... less in it... »

As children we believed the world to be gigantic. Leaving the house felt like going on a quest, journeying abroad like being on an adventure, and the dreams of taking a spaceship to explore the universe seemed within the realm of possibilities...

As adults we come to the realisation that despite its size, a lifetime is not enough to explore all possibilities offered to us by this huge world. This disillusion forces us to focus only on a select few things, while being constrained by the time consumed by obligations. We navigate through life in hope of finding treasures, if we're lucky enough to find something worthy in these degrading times.

To make it more challenging, we gave birth to a beast that we may not be able to tame. The kraken is devouring everything on its trail : text, images, videos, code, games, economy, society, environment, thoughts, humanity...

Some may argue that it is only a mere tool and that they're still their master, but are they really ? They trust it blindly, delegating every decision, abdicating privacy and critical thinking, and offering heart and soul to what is supposedly their slave. Don't they realize by doing so that they are the ones being willingly enslaved ? But everyone is free to renounce their freedom, it's still their choice after all.

What I find frightening is moreso that the kraken will eventually spread its tentacles in every aspect of our lives and society, and we know for a fact that humans cannot be trusted to remain reasonable and rational when it comes to their own interests. Imagine a world where any evidence can be forged, history can be rewritten to fit narratives, truth can be altered and undistinguishable from lies, masses can be manipulated at a scalable level, any dissidence can be tracked and alienated... A totalitarian regime leaving no hope for rebellion.

The shores on which we're currently living in may become remnants of the past, and the irony is that they may even disappear from any map. The ship has already sailed, all sailors need to deal with it, even if it means feeding the kraken with their own crew and ship parts.

■ The words in the sky
🇺🇸
🇫🇷
Saturday, August 29, 2026
I find the Chinese character for sky « 天 » fascinating.

The sky overlooks us all, including the tallest of people (大) hence the stroke above them.
It also separates our world in two: the lower world in which we live (天下), and the upper world where the gods reside (天上).
The sky even has its own language and writing, which humans study through astronomy (天文).

Speaking of stars, they are used to designate the days of the week in Chinese (星期).
The sun and the moon designate respectively the day (日) and the months (月).
Yet the year (年) is not based on the content of the sky but on something more down-to-earth: the harvest of cereals (禾).
A bit disappointing to break the pattern, but it is true that it is difficult to directly observe the Earth's revolution around the Sun, and agriculture is quite necessary to not starve so its importance is understandable. However, by pushing just a little further we can actually put all the pieces back together...

Just as the moon influences the tides, the sun influences the soil indirectly through the seasons. The Chinese therefore combined a first character that can be linked to the harvest with the character for sky to form the seasons. Their modern transcription requires a bit of imagination to understand the etymology of each, but for example the word for autumn has remained quite equivocal. « 秋 » [cereals (禾) and fire (火)], does not refer to the flamboyant color of the leaves, but to the agricultural practice of burning certain parts of the fields in order to fight against parasitic insects, which proliferated with the arrival of autumn.

I like to imagine our solar system as a giant clock, even if this is not a very unique idea since all civilizations have already had this thought. In French, the days of the week are based on the planets: Lundi (Moon), Mardi (Mars), Mercredi (Mercury), Jeudi (Jupiter), Vendredi (Venus) and Samedi (Saturn). English on the other hand only kept Monday (Moon), Saturday (Saturn) and Sunday (Sun).

I have already shared my thoughts on (Western) astrology in the past, but perhaps this is the right time to detail my vision a bit more. If predicting the future seems really far-fetched to me, deducing behavior from the date of birth seems more credible if we assume this concept of « seed ». Correlations and causality are surely intertwined, but knowing that the development of children strongly influences their adult personality as psychology explains, it is not so foolish to think that the time of year in which a child is born can have an impact on them. After all, the seasons influence our lifestyle habits, our surroundings, our general mood, the available foods, the amount of sunlight received, etc., but they cannot predict accidents, traumas suffered, etc. Perhaps this is just an imprecise Laplace's Demon.

But the question has never been whether astrology had any scientific basis or not, but rather to be interested in why these interpretations actually exist. It is easy to throw under the bus all the conspiracy theorists, fanatics and extremists, but the truth is that most people don't even make the effort to try to understand concepts they reject. Playing the devil's advocate is a good exercise to keep the mind busy.

■ You and me
🇺🇸
🇫🇷
Monday, August 3, 2026

I generally avoid sharing the same artist (and even more so when it comes to the same track) but I felt compelled to make an exception this time.

I already shared the studio version a few months ago, but I was obliged to share the demo version as soon as I listened to it. In fact, I might even go as far as to say that listening to both versions offers a unique experience that cannot be obtained by listening to just one of them.

It's the same band, the same melody, the same lyrics, and yet... The feeling is completely different.

If we approach the track from the perspective of relationships, the studio version has this « honeymoon » vibe carried by the more polished production and richer instrumental, while also having this aspect of naivety, that moment when we think everything will last forever. But paradoxically, the « polished » rendering also has this slightly superficial and perhaps even artificial tint, where we mask reality to make it more beautiful than it possibly is.

In contrast, the demo version only keeps a modest piano for the melody and a clean voice, offering this sincere aspect and a suspended moment where everything superfluous has been erased. The beauty of this version lies in the fact that the perceived emotional color can change entirely. It can have this melancholic, nostalgic and perhaps even a little sad tint for someone who has just gone through a breakup, while someone in a mature and hardened relationship will find intimacy, complicity and authenticity in it. But it is not necessary to oppose the two versions, after all when we really love something or someone, we start to appreciate all of their different versions.

It is possible to view these tracks from a more introspective perspective, and to be completely honest, that's more how I experience them. « You » doesn't necessarily have to be interpreted as the « Other », it can also be another version of « Me », and that, in my opinion, amplifies greatly the depth of the song. It echoes a bit my previous post, where I was exploring the constancy in change.

This vulnerable and true version of ourselves that we never fully show, and that hides behind more romanticized, embellished or sometimes even faked versions that we show to others, is similar to how this studio version is what we play on the radio while the demo version is hidden in extended plays.

Or maybe this demo is like a broken or imperfect version of ourselves that, after being rebuilt or completed, becomes the most accomplished version that we are proud to show to the rest of the world ?

Or maybe this demo is to remind us that there used to be a time when we made our lives way less complicated, and that simplicity is enough on its own ?

Lovers who split or reconcile each other, versions of ourselves that confront or complement each other, interpretations of the same melody that question or respond to each other... That's why I love this track so much, because it offers a multiplicity in the duplicity that evolve with each listen and life experience.

Maybe it's the smell of the rain, hearing the drops crashing on my window in rhythm with the piano notes, and the fact that it's that moment of the night that I'm already borrowing time to the following day, that make me think of all these trivialities ? Sometimes I kinda resent myself for not being able to just appreciate things as they are without trying to give them some meaning, but I guess that's just how it is ?

■ Behind the dunes
🇺🇸
🇫🇷
Sunday, July 19, 2026

I stumbled upon this music by chance at night and instantly fell in love with it... In fact, it really brings together all the elements I love.

The title is quite vague so that we can let ourselves be carried away by our imagination when we close our eyes. And a different painting unfolds with each play.

The chants in an unknown language blur our temporal references. Is it an ancestral language that we have completely lost understanding of ? Or is it a language that has become unrecognizable due to the erosion of time ?

The musical structure itself is like a story. The flute begins its journey alone, before being joined by traveling companions with whom it shares its melody of life. The choirs are added to give an almost providential dimension : the legend is written as it is told. Time passes and the melody of the beginning leads to new harmonies, despite the instruments remaining the same. Their adventure has definitely transformed them, yet the melodies of the past eventually resonate again, is it nostalgia or introspection ?

The piece ultimately concludes as it began: a flute singing alone and possibly abandoned by its companions. In the final moments, is the opening melody there to remind us of the journey we've taken since the beginning or a reminder that our hearts remain the same despite all the metamorphoses we've undergone ?

This track reminded me of two series that marked my childhood: Tsubasa Reservoir Chronicles and Pandora Hearts. Even though I haven't seen the anime adaptations, I still listened to the soundtracks (especially to listen to the melody of « Lacie ») and it turns out that Yuki Kajiura had worked on both, hence the similar style. The track was co-written by Revo who composed for Bravely Default, one of my favorite games. I suppose that's why this music seems exquisite to me.

The chants also remind me of the language of Chaos in the NieR series, and in the same way as « Ashes of Dream », a « transcribed » version exists, titled « Sajin no Kanata e・・・ ». The melody is practically identical, however the lyrics are in Japanese, and to be honest the text is particularly beautiful.

This kind of diptych simply makes me think that humans are really universal... We like to think that we are unique, yet throughout the ages and cultures, everyone asks themselves the same questions, and no one has yet found any real answers to them.

■ The hive
🇺🇸
🇫🇷
Tuesday, July 14, 2026

Bees appear to us as clouds :
Everyone sees what they want in them.

If the French Revolution was undoubtedly a major identity crisis for the people of France, oscillating between monarchy, republic and empire, the search for legitimacy supported by biomimicry is an aside of our history that I find fascinating.

There is an undeniable advantage to use nature as a reference, as it appears to us as a universal truth. Some may see it as the meticulous work of God while others will see it as the inevitable result of evolution.

Ants and bees are social insects that have long inspired us for multiple reasons. Just like us, they live in society and are able to cooperate to achieve a common goal.

In a hive, society is perfectly organized:

  • A supreme authority that governs community life while ensuring continuity
  • A strict hierarchy, well-defined roles and an absence of rule-breaking
  • Disciplined production, collective work and efficient logistics
  • Armed defense with painful stingers to protect the community
  • Ingenious architecture with captivating geometric constructions

Some will see the end of the analogy where these insect societies are led by a female rather than a male, and yet, most of the ideals we aspire to appear to us as feminine figures: freedom, justice, victory and truth to name a few, so is it really that surprising ? (Nota Bene : in French, these are « feminine » words, but even in arts, these concepts are often personified as women).

The prism through which we perceive apian societies is actually influenced by our own beliefs.

Is it a monarchy or an empire ?
Do we see a group serving a tyrannical sovereign ? Or rather siblings under the governance of a benevolent mother ?

Is it a republic or a democracy ?
Bees can rebel to the point of executing their queen when she is no longer deemed fit to govern the hive... Meaning that the mass alone decides the legitimacy of the one who governs them.

Feminists will find comfort in the fact that nature has chosen to place a woman in a position of power, communists will see in it that all work and production must be collectivized for the common good, monarchists will have a proof that a regency is necessary because it is the natural order of things, while adepts of the republic will retain that a people only lend power to their leaders.

Everything is therefore a matter of narrative, but everyone seeks justifications for their own morality.

And you, what do bees inspire in you ?
